## Short Project Pitch

QuantNews Updater is the delivery layer for quant research monitoring: it
fetches fresh papers, tracks what is new, ranks what matters, and outputs a
digest you can use internally or publish as a newsletter.

## README One-Liner

Quant research monitor and newsletter generator for turning fresh arXiv papers
into ranked, structured digests.

## Example Social Post

Built and open-sourced a small quant research updater that polls arXiv, tracks
new papers, ranks them by theme, and turns them into newsletter-style digests.
Started as a practical application layer after exploring QuantMind.

## First Newsletter Positioning

- Market microstructure research digest
- Quant execution and TCA briefing

- LLMs in finance research monitor
